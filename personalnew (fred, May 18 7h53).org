**** Summary
This paper presents 3 distance metrices between spatial fields based
on mathematical morphology concepts of erosion and dilation. THey also
present the connection between the different metrices as well as some
general properties of interest.
The authors also present an index to rank a set of spatial fields
based on their similarity

- page 1 spatial is repeated in first paragraph, second column
- page 5 crossreference is missing.
- page 6 last paragraph crossreference has a typo
- page 7 the paragraph before table 5 is not clear. Seem to be missing
  something in the grammar.
- fig 3 has very low resolution. need to be higher
- table 6-8 difficult to read. font is too small
- page 8 line 40 matrixes=matrices
- Section 5 title mention classification but in the discussion of this
  section I don't see any actual classification algorithm. I would
  expect to see a way to group the fields in a specific number of classes.

the advantages of using these metrics over other metrics. What does
these metrics provide that other metrics do not.

appliclation for classification which would seem like a good
example. I wished they had implemented this in the paper.
